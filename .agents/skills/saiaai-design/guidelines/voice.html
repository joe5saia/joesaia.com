How SaiaAI writes

Person

Third-person "SaiaAI" / "Joe". Address the reader as "you / your business". Never "we".

Tone

Calm, senior, plain-spoken. Confident without hype or exclamation.

Casing

Sentence case in prose & headings. UPPERCASE only for eyebrows & buttons. Headlines end in a period.

Vocabulary

understand · practical · high-leverage · boutique · maintainable · avoid unnecessary spend.

Say this

The smallest high-quality solution that creates real leverage.

Not this

Revolutionary AI to 10x your business overnight! 🚀
